Capabilities

Look established from day one

First-year awards programmes compete with ones that have run for a decade. The presentation has to carry the same weight.

Branded Microsites

Your logo, your colours, your domain. The public site carries your identity end to end — nominees, voting, tickets, gallery and blog all wearing your brand, not a vendor's.

Email Campaigns

Reusable templates with variable substitution and live preview, custom blasts per event, scheduled delivery, and a resend for the addresses that bounced the first time.

SMS Blasts

Reach the audience that never opens email. Reusable SMS templates with preview, blasts to your event contacts, and delivery tracking on every message sent.

Content & Blog

Publish posts with categories and tags, feature the ones that matter, and moderate comments before they appear. Static pages cover About, Terms and anything else you need.

Media Library

One place for every asset — upload, download and remove images and documents used across the microsite, blog posts and event pages.

Campaign Reporting

A unified notification log across email and SMS — status, recipient and timestamp on every send — filterable and exportable when a sponsor asks what their money bought.

Branding and marketing, answered

The questions that come up when a programme wants to own its presentation.

No. White-labelling is complete — your logo, your colours and your own custom domain across every public page, and branded PDF tickets to match. Visitors see your awards programme, not the platform underneath it.
It builds itself. Event registrations and ticket purchases backfill into a central contacts list that spans all of your events, and you can export it to Excel at any point. Nominee phonebooks export separately for SMS to nominees and their networks.
Yes. Email blasts support scheduled delivery, so the "voting is open" message goes out at eight in the morning without anyone being awake to press send. You can also resend to recipients a first attempt failed to reach.
