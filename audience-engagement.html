Capabilities

Interaction without the friction

Nobody downloads an app for a ten-second poll. Everything here runs in the browser your audience already has open.

Live Polls

Publish a poll live and results move as votes land. Close it the moment you have your answer, and the room sees the final split on screen.

Digital Surveys

Multiple-choice or single-choice questions, reordered as you like, with bulk option entry when you are building something long. Public page, no login for respondents.

Interactive Voting

Audience choice awards, people's favourite, best-dressed — any moment where the room should decide. The same voting engine that runs your main categories.

Response Analytics

Counts and percentages per option, updating as responses arrive, with the full result set exportable to Excel when you want it in the post-event report.

Reusable Polls

Duplicate a poll for the next session, or reset its responses and run the same questions with a different room. Build the instrument once, use it all year.

Attached or Standalone

Hang a poll off a specific event, or run it entirely on its own for research and community feedback between events. A single dashboard shows all of them.

How It Works

Question to answer in four steps

Quick enough to build between sessions, which is usually when you decide you need one.

1
Build

Write the questions

Choose single or multiple choice, add your options — one at a time or in bulk — and reorder until the sequence reads the way you would ask it aloud.

2
Publish

Put it live

Publish to the public poll page on your microsite. Attach it to an event, or run it standalone for community feedback between events.

3
Collect

Watch it fill

Responses arrive from phones in the room and counts move in real time, with percentages per option ready to put on the screen behind the stage.

4
Close

Lock the result

Close the poll to stop responses, export the analytics to Excel, then duplicate or reset it when you need the same questions again.
