Capabilities

Everything the awards cycle asks for

From the first nomination form to the winner reveal on gala night — built for programs that have to stand up to scrutiny.

Online Nominations

Public nomination forms per event and per category, organised into nomination groups. Open and close each category independently, then lock the form when nominations end.

Multi-Channel Voting

USSD on any handset, M-Pesa STK push, Paystack card and bank, plus PawaPay and InstaSend mobile money. Vote bundles let you sell pay-to-vote packs at any price point.

Jury & Panel Voting

Generate one-time or reusable voting codes and issue them to judges, so a panel ballot stays closed while public voting runs in the same category set.

Real-Time Monitoring

A live statistics dashboard showing vote totals, revenue and top candidates as they move, with percentage breakdowns per category the moment votes land.

Vote Integrity

Every vote is logged with voter, time, channel and amount. Track sessions per voter, invalidate suspicious votes, and reconcile pending USSD transactions chunk by chunk.

Results & Winners

Calculate results per category, export to Excel and PDF, mark individual winners and announce them category by category when you are ready — not a moment before.

Nominations

A nomination pipeline that manages itself

Entries arrive through a branded public form and land in a unified inbox that spans every event you run. Review them one at a time or clear a backlog in a single bulk action, then promote your finalists straight into a voting category.

  • Transfer a nominee between categories without re-entry
  • Export the nominee list to Excel, per category

How It Works

Four phases, one dashboard

The platform mirrors the natural awards journey. You control which phase each category sits in, and nothing opens until you say so.

1
Set Up

Build the categories

Create categories and sub-categories, group them for navigation, and decide which ones take public nominations versus jury selection.

2
Nominate

Open nominations

Publish the nomination form, review entries as they arrive, and promote your approved finalists into voting categories with one action.

3
Vote

Open voting

Assign nominee codes, switch on the channels you want, and watch totals, revenue and leaders move on the live dashboard.

4
Announce

Reveal the winners

Close voting, reconcile any pending transactions, calculate final results, and announce winners category by category on your schedule.

In most African awards programmes USSD is the single largest voting channel.
Every vote is written to an audit log with the voter, timestamp, channel and amount, and sessions are tracked per voter. Suspicious votes can be invalidated individually and results recalculated immediately, so the published figures always match the ledger behind them.
Yes. Voting categories are independent of nomination categories, and you can generate one-time or reusable voting codes to issue to your judges. That keeps a panel ballot closed to invited voters while public voting runs in parallel on the categories you choose.
You define vote bundles — pay-to-vote packs such as five votes for KES 50 — and set the price per bundle. Voters pay through M-Pesa STK push, Paystack, PawaPay or InstaSend, and revenue lands in the finance dashboard with commission and payout tracking already applied.
USSD transactions can settle after the session ends, so there is a dedicated reconciliation tool that works through pending votes chunk by chunk. You run it after closing a category and before calculating results, which means no legitimate vote is dropped and no duplicate is counted.
