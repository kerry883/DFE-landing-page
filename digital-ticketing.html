Capabilities

Ticketing that survives a real door

Built for events where several hundred people arrive in the same twenty minutes and every one of them expects to walk straight in.

Flexible Ticket Types

Early Bird, VIP, Regular, table of ten — as many types as your event needs, each with its own price, quantity cap and sale window. Switch one off without deleting its sales history.

Promo Codes

Percentage or fixed-amount discounts with usage limits and expiry dates, plus per-code analytics so you know which partner or influencer actually shifted tickets.

QR Code Ticketing

Every ticket carries a unique QR code generated at purchase. It is the only thing the gate needs, and it cannot be reused once it has been scanned in.

Instant Delivery

A branded PDF ticket is generated and emailed the moment payment clears. Resend it to a buyer who lost it, or void one without issuing a refund.

Check-In & Gate Control

Scan arrivals one by one or check in a group at once. The check-in report records arrival time and gate, so you can see exactly how the door flowed.

Transfers & Refunds

Buyers can hand a ticket to someone else and you approve or reject the transfer. Refunds run through the same admin workflow, tracked from request to completion.

Selling

Price it the way you actually sell it

Early Bird closes on a date, VIP is capped at forty, corporate tables only go on sale once the sponsors have confirmed. Each ticket type carries its own price, cap and sale window, so the rules enforce themselves instead of living in someone's head.

  • Unlimited ticket types per event
  • Quantity caps that close a tier automatically when it sells out
  • Sale windows that open and close on schedule
  • Toggle a type off without deleting its records

Payments

Pay the way your audience already pays

A card-only checkout loses most of an African audience at the first screen. Buyers here pay by M-Pesa STK push, card or bank through Paystack, or regional mobile money through PawaPay and InstaSend — whichever they actually have.

  • M-Pesa STK push — payment prompt straight to the phone
  • Paystack for Visa, Mastercard, bank transfer and USSD
  • PawaPay and InstaSend for pan-African mobile money
  • Gateway fees passed to the buyer or absorbed, per event

How It Works

From on sale to the door

Four steps, and the only manual work left is deciding what to charge.

1
Configure

Set your ticket types

Create each tier with its price, quantity cap and sale window, then add any promo codes you want partners and sponsors to distribute.

2
Sell

Open checkout

Buyers pick their tickets on your microsite and pay by mobile money, card or bank. Revenue lands in the finance dashboard as it comes in.

3
Deliver

Tickets go out instantly

A QR-coded PDF is generated and emailed on payment. Buyers can request a transfer, and you approve or reject it from admin.

4
Admit

Scan them in

Work the gate with scan or bulk check-in, then export the attendee list, check-in report and sales report before you go home.
