Capabilities

Everything the platform cannot do for you

The parts of an awards night that still need people — organised inside the same system that runs the rest.

Photography & Media

Coverage on the night and press engagement around it, with everything shot landing in your event's photo albums and video gallery rather than on somebody's hard drive.

Sponsor & Partner Management

Sponsors recorded with their logos and tier levels, surfaced across the microsite and programme, so the visibility they were sold is the visibility they get.

Venue Coordination

Venues held with their address, capacity and map link on the public site, so guests can find the room and you can see whether it holds the list you have sold.

Supplier Coordination

Staging, sound, lighting, catering and décor lined up against your run sheet, with one point of contact instead of nine numbers in your phone.

Gallery & Documents

Photo albums and a separate video gallery on the microsite, plus programme booklets and PDFs attached to the event for guests to download.

Promotion & Visibility

Getting the programme in front of the right audience before the night, and keeping it visible afterwards through the gallery and coverage people actually share.

Every supplier on one board

The week before an event is when things go wrong — the venue expects a different layout, the sound company thinks they arrive at four, nobody confirmed the lectern. One coordinator, one list, and a run sheet everyone is working from.

How It Works

How we work alongside you

You keep the relationships and the creative decisions. We carry the coordination load.

1
Scope

Understand the night

We go through your format, expected numbers, venue shortlist and sponsor commitments, and work out what support the event actually needs.

2
Assemble

Line up the suppliers

Venue, staging, sound, lighting, catering and photography booked and confirmed against your dates, with one coordinator holding the thread.

3
Deliver

Run the evening

We work the run sheet on the night — load-in, red carpet, sponsor placements and the award sequence — while your team hosts the room.

4
Wrap

Publish and hand back

Coverage goes up in your gallery, sponsors get their assets, and the event record closes with its documents and reports attached.
